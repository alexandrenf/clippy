# Clippy iOS Brand Specification

## Direction

Editorial utility: a quiet paper-like workspace that keeps lists primary and sync machinery secondary. The clipped-paper app mark is the single signature detail.

## Color tokens

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `#F3F0EA` | `#171817` | Screen background |
| Paper | `#FFFDF9` | `#252624` | Lists and controls |
| Field | `#ECE9E2` | `#2E302D` | Secondary input rows |
| Ink | `#1C1D1B` | `#F5F2EB` | Primary text |
| Muted ink | `#676A65` | `#A9ACA5` | Supporting text |
| Hairline | `#DEDAD1` | `#3A3C38` | Separators |
| Clippy blue | `#3478B8` | `#82B7E8` | Primary actions and active state |
| Powder blue | `#E2EDF7` | `#263A4B` | Selection and status backgrounds |
| Success | `#287B55` | `#69C798` | Synced state |
| Warning | `#A8641C` | `#E6A45F` | Sync in progress |

## Typography

- Display: Apple system serif, 30 pt bold, for `Clippy` and `Lists`.
- Heading: SF Pro, 17 pt semibold, for list and card titles.
- Body: SF Pro, 16 pt regular, for content and input.
- Caption: SF Pro, 12 pt regular or semibold, for sync and metadata.

## Geometry

- Spacing grid: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32 pt.
- Screen inset: 16 pt.
- Tap targets: at least 44 pt.
- Radii: 8 pt small, 12 pt controls, 16 pt surfaces.
- Shadows: black at 5.5% opacity, soft and low.

## Rules

- Lists are the visual focus; sync status stays one compact line.
- Use Clippy blue only for actions, active marks, and the paperclip.
- No gradients, emoji icons, left accent bars, heavy borders, or forced color scheme.
- Use SF Symbols in a consistent medium or semibold weight.
